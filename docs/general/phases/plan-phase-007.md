## Purpose and Ownership

This phase completes the launch commerce and operational-control boundary. It permits only guaranteed original cosmetics and declared non-power convenience, proves that every eligible Marketplace receipt grants exactly once and restores after interruption, applies policy and age-suitability checks before prompting, reconciles revenue evidence to durable grants, emits privacy-safe product and gameplay analytics, validates eligible launch leaderboards, retains bounded moderation and appeal evidence, and gives least-privileged operators versioned feature, maintenance, alert, and rollback controls.

The phase canonically owns `CORE-REQ-021` and `CORE-REQ-022`. Analytics service owns schema registration, redaction, sampling, reconciliation, dashboards, and health evidence. The purchase boundary owns product eligibility and receipt decisions. Policy service owns policy evaluation. Transaction and Profile services remain the only authorities for durable grants. Leaderboard service owns `OrderedDataStore` projections, not source profile state. Security service owns bounded anomaly evidence and quarantine recommendations, not automatic irreversible punishment. Release operations own controlled configuration application, maintenance, and rollback actions through audited permissions.

The master plan owns product scope, requirement meaning, global topology, external prerequisites, owner decisions, and the completion endpoint. This file owns only dependency-ordered execution of `CORE-PHASE-007`. It cannot authorize paid random gameplay power, purchasable odds, gameplay currency sales, inventory recovery charges, final public publication, Phase 008 candidate certification, or Phase 009 rollout. The destructive production purchase smoke is required for phase closure but may run only within the exact authorization bound by `EXT-010`.

## Architecture and Implementation Boundaries

### Ownership and Dependency Direction

- Analytics service accepts typed domain events after the authoritative service transition. It cannot decide rewards, grants, scores, ownership, policy eligibility, moderation penalties, or feature state.
- The purchase boundary validates product and policy state and maps Marketplace receipts to Transaction service operations. Transaction and Profile services alone commit durable grants and revisions. The client and analytics destination never become grant evidence.
- Policy service exposes only a bounded eligibility decision and stable reason to product presentation and diagnostics. Raw platform policy payloads remain inside the server policy boundary and are not general event properties.
- Leaderboard service receives normalized projections from authoritative domain state and writes them asynchronously. `OrderedDataStore` is not the source of inventory, currency, score history, duel results, or moderation state.
- Security service produces bounded signals and reversible quarantine decisions. Moderation reviewers act through permission-checked tools. Neither Analytics nor Leaderboard service may silently punish or mutate source profiles.
- Feature and maintenance consumers use one validated versioned configuration projection. Services cannot invent local defaults that reopen a disabled path. Recovery interfaces are explicit exceptions and remain narrower than normal mutation APIs.

### Product Manifest, Originality, and Policy

- Each manifest entry has exactly one stable internal product key and one exact Roblox product identity supplied by `EXT-013`. A retired key or platform ID cannot later identify a different grant.
- The guaranteed grant definition uses stable catalog or entitlement IDs and a version. Display labels are not ownership identities. A manifest update cannot reinterpret an already committed receipt.
- Cosmetic products reference the Phase 003 original asset manifest and provenance review. Product copy, iconography, previews, reveal effects, audio, and age suitability must pass the same originality and Community Standards boundary as the game.
- Declared convenience cannot add a saved loadout or affect the active deck's fifty-two slots, five active Jokers, score stages, odds, pity, reward multipliers, currencies, matchmaking, board value, trade eligibility, recovery priority, or moderation treatment.
- Server eligibility combines enabled state, product mapping, applicable policy, ownership or repeatability, supported region, age suitability, service availability, and manifest version. Any unknown required input fails closed.
- The client receives exact eligible product copy and current platform-confirmed price projection. It submits only purchase intent for an offered product key. It cannot override product ID, price, grant, policy, eligibility, or receipt outcome.

### Receipt, Grant, and Restore State

- Receipt processing distinguishes received, validation pending, grant pending, committed, and quarantined conflict states. Presentation acknowledgement is not a durable state.
- The platform receipt identity and immutable product mapping determine one operation identity. Identical replay returns the original committed grant result. A repeated receipt with conflicting product or player information does not grant and enters bounded review.
- The durable receipt stores only the minimum identifiers, manifest and grant versions, operation identity, result hash, profile revision, timestamps needed for retry and audit, and stable state or error code. It does not store payment credentials or raw platform payloads.
- If the profile is unavailable, locked elsewhere, read-only, migrating, or temporarily unsavable, the processor preserves a retry result and grants nothing until the durable commit succeeds.
- Product disablement blocks new prompts. It does not discard a valid outstanding receipt, erase an entitlement, or mint a replacement operation. Unsafe cosmetic presentation may fall back neutrally while ownership remains intact.
- Operator remediation may inspect and retry a bounded receipt state through the same idempotent operation. It cannot create a synthetic receipt, change the grant, directly edit arbitrary inventory, or mark success without durable proof.

### Analytics Schemas, Privacy, and Sampling

- Every event has a stable ID, schema version, authoritative producer, timestamp source, environment and build identity, bounded property types, privacy class, sampling mode, and retention class.
- Onboarding steps, pack commits and choices, card or Joker acquisition, deck mutations, Arcana, Grade, salvage, durable rewards, currency flows, duel results, persisted trade states, purchases, receipts, profile errors, security signals, moderation decisions, feature changes, maintenance, and rollback are unsampled.
- High-frequency non-economic presentation, performance, preview, or input-quality events may be deterministically sampled. The inclusion function, rate, weight, schema version, and configuration version are recorded. Repeating the same event identity produces the same inclusion decision.
- The event pipeline batches within platform limits and uses bounded queues. Analytics failure cannot block or roll back gameplay, a receipt grant, or prepared recovery. Critical evidence remains in its authoritative receipt, journal, profile, configuration, or audit store and raises an alert when analytics ingestion is unavailable.
- Events may include stable content, action, operation, receipt, trade, board, reason, version, categorical band, count, and duration fields only when the schema allows them. They exclude raw profiles, inventory arrays, hidden hands, credentials, policy payloads, unfiltered text, arbitrary error strings, and exploit instructions.

### Economy and Commerce Reconciliation

- Each currency event includes currency ID, integer delta, previous balance, resulting balance, reason ID, operation identity, profile revision, and bounded context from the authoritative transaction result.
- Each purchase record links receipt identity, product key and platform ID, grant version, operation identity, entitlement or owned result, profile revision, feature and policy versions, and analytics event identity.
- Reconciliation statuses distinguish exact committed, retryable pending, quarantined conflict, and approved remediated outcomes. A missing analytics event does not erase the authoritative grant, but it is a reconciliation defect that must be repaired or explained before closure.
- Authorized production proof requires exact one-to-one receipt and durable-grant agreement. Any confirmed duplicate grant, unhandled eligible receipt, orphan grant, lost entitlement, or unexplained revenue difference is a critical release blocker.

### Leaderboards

- The board registry records stable board ID, display purpose, authoritative source field or calculation, normalization, direction, season namespace, update rule, feature state, eligibility, tie behavior, page bounds, and reward class.
- Launch may enable only implemented source-backed boards such as forms discovered, forms currently owned, Deck Set mastery, best card output, Formation Rush seasonal score, and total Cash earned when each passes its exact source and privacy tests. The registry, not this list, records the final enabled subset for the frozen candidate.
- Boss contribution remains disabled under `FUT-007`. Fair and Power ratings remain disabled because Phase 005 supplies no rating write and population-supported ratings remain `FUT-008` unless a later explicit plan revision promotes them.
- Board writes use server-generated finite bounded normalized numbers, never formatted strings or client values. An older or lower stale update cannot overwrite a newer authoritative best where the board contract is monotonic.
- A write failure preserves the profile result and queues only a bounded idempotent retry. Quarantined or impossible state is excluded from public projection while evidence remains available for review. Clearing quarantine triggers a source-derived recomputation, not reuse of client values.
- Ties show shared rank where supported. Rewards, if any are already in approved launch configuration, are cosmetic or title presentation only and cannot grant exclusive power.

### Moderation Evidence and Appeals

- Moderation records contain stable subject, reason, source, severity band, corroboration count, bounded evidence references, catalog, configuration, schema, build and place versions, reversible action, operator action, appeal state, and audit timestamps as applicable.
- Records do not contain raw profile dumps, full inventories, unfiltered player text, authentication material, arbitrary client payloads, or instructions that teach exploitation.
- Automatic responses are limited to rejecting an action, applying configured rate limits, withholding an invalid board projection, or quarantining impossible state for review. One uncorroborated signal cannot permanently ban, delete ownership, erase currency, destroy a receipt, or irreversibly alter progress.
- Operator decisions require a dedicated scope and audit receipt. Appeal evidence binds the original reason, immutable bounded evidence references, decision history, and resolution; later review never rewrites the original record.
- Retention and access are versioned and documented. Analytics summaries use aggregate reason counts, not the private moderation record.

### Feature Controls, Maintenance, and Prepared Recovery

- Each risky action declares its feature ID, safe default, prerequisites, normal behavior, disabled behavior, in-flight behavior, recovery behavior, player copy, diagnostic fields, and owner scope.
- Individual content, Deck Set packs, Joker and Arcana offers, acquisition, Formation and task reward commits, salvage, Arcana application, Grade upgrades, passive and offline rewards, duel rewards, new trades, rare announcements, purchase prompts, receipt grants, restores, and individual products are represented. A global economy gate and maintenance state provide defense in depth.
- A disabled new-acquisition path cannot start an operation. A committed or durable pending operation follows its exact recovery contract. Pending pack contents remain recoverable; a prepared trade journal continues reconciliation; an eligible paid receipt remains retryable; existing ownership is never deleted.
- Remote configuration is validated, versioned, atomically applied, cached, and exposed in diagnostics. A missing, stale beyond policy, malformed, or unknown critical configuration uses the documented safe state, normally closing new risky acquisition and mutation while preserving recovery.
- Maintenance presentation distinguishes temporary unavailability from success or loss, offers safe retry or leave, and does not create a blank overwrite. Read-only mode stops new rewards and mutations but permits safe inspection and required server-side recovery.

### Operator Permissions, Alerts, and Rollback

- Permission scopes separate health viewing, moderation review, commerce reconciliation, feature changes, maintenance changes, and rollback execution. Possessing one does not imply another.
- Operator requests identify environment, action, target scope, expected current configuration version, request identity, reason code, and bounded parameters. Stale or conflicting versions reject without partial application.
- Every allowed and denied operator action produces an audit receipt. No interface accepts arbitrary executable code, arbitrary DataStore keys, credentials, raw profile export, or unrestricted inventory mutation.
- Alerts use stable IDs and exact versioned definitions. Zero-tolerance critical conditions include confirmed item or entitlement duplication, unhandled eligible production receipt, orphan paid grant, unexplained receipt-to-grant divergence, blocked prepared trade recovery, unauthorized operator action, and rollback invariant failure.
- Launch metric alerts use the master thresholds with explicit environment, population, aggregation window, minimum sample handling, and stale-data behavior. A missing or stale dashboard is not evidence of health.
- Rollback uses exact current and prior-known-good versions, applies atomically or fails closed, preserves committed profiles and ownership, never replays with a new operation identity, and retains receipts, journals, audit records, configuration identity, and diagnostic evidence.

### Performance and Compatibility

- Analytics and board work is batched and bounded. It does not write DataStore on every click or block hot gameplay paths. High-frequency task input and frame loops are summarized rather than emitted individually.
- Product keys, receipt identities, grant versions, entitlement IDs, event and reason IDs, board and season IDs, moderation reason and action IDs, feature IDs, maintenance states, alert IDs, operator action IDs, and rollback manifest versions are compatibility surfaces.
- Schema migrations are pure, repeatable, fixture-tested, and ownership-preserving. A migration cannot reinterpret a committed product or receipt.
- Every diagnostic and completion artifact binds source commit, build, catalog, configuration, product, grant, receipt, analytics, board, moderation, feature, alert, runbook, environment, and place versions needed to reproduce it.
