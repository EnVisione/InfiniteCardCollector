## Purpose and Ownership

This phase turns the complete launch collection and product surfaces delivered through Phase 003 into the repeatable active and return loop. A blank profile must open its first free five-card pack in less than thirty seconds, reach a real card-level choice in less than forty-five seconds, understand the fifty-two-slot deck, and complete the first guided Formation Rush in less than two minutes. Formation Rush must then operate as a complete server-authoritative sixty-second round with an eight-card hand, one-to-five-card submissions, all twelve formations, redraw, discard and reshuffle behavior, streaks, prompts, deterministic calculation traces, and exactly-once rewards.

The phase also delivers Suit Sorter, Sequence Bench, Arcana Trace, and Grade Press through one server-owned task protocol; durable timed boosts; bounded passive and offline return; daily goals; collection rewards; Deck Set affinity and mastery progress; and the next-goal presentation that connects these systems. Active play remains the primary progression path. Frozen representative fixtures must prove ordinary active play earns at least five times the normalized passive baseline, and no unattended, held-input, replay, clock manipulation, or reconnect path may become the optimum.

The phase canonically owns `CORE-REQ-015`, `CORE-REQ-016`, `CORE-REQ-017`, and `CORE-REQ-018`. The Formation service owns transient Formation Rush authority and phase integration. Task, Boost, Collection, Transaction, Profile, Analytics, Security, Deck, Joker, Pack, and client components retain their service boundaries. The master plan owns product scope, requirement meaning, global order, decisions, prerequisites, and completion authority. This file owns only the dependency-ordered execution of `CORE-PHASE-004`; it cannot revise the protected plan set, promote later social or release scope, or start `CORE-PHASE-005` before this phase is merged and tagged.

## Architecture and Implementation Boundaries

The Phase 001 shared calculation and configuration layer remains pure and authoritative for formation detection, score stages, affinity math, caps, and reward values. Phase 004 services orchestrate those contracts; they do not duplicate formation tables, Joker logic, rank order, currency values, or balance formulas. Shared data and runtime types belong under `ReplicatedStorage/Shared`. Authoritative services remain under `ServerScriptService/Server/Services`. Controllers under `StarterPlayerScripts/Client/Controllers` submit narrow intent and render snapshots or patches. Product surfaces under `StarterGui/App` do not access profile storage or decide outcomes.

The server state flow for Formation Rush is ordered:

1. `formation.start` passes the network gateway, profile readiness, writable state, rate, eligibility, and duplicate-request checks.
2. Formation service creates the authoritative round ID and seed, derives the ordered deck snapshot, constructs draw and discard piles, fills the eight-card hand with Blanks only when necessary, and sends a bounded snapshot.
3. Redraw and submission requests name the current round and current server-hand instances. The service rejects stale, duplicate, foreign, out-of-hand, over-rate, late, or invalid-count intent before changing round state.
4. A valid submission calls the frozen pure detector and score pipeline with an explicitly ordered normalized input. The result selects one configured best base formation and only declared stackable bonuses, applies Joker, affinity, boost, and mode stages in the frozen order, and produces a trace.
5. Used cards enter discard, the hand refills deterministically, streak and prompt state update, and the client receives the authoritative transition. Presentation timing cannot change state.
6. At deadline, the round enters resolution exactly once. Formation service submits accumulated eligible outputs to Transaction service. A durable operation records the result, profile revision, reasons, and recovery reference before success is shown.

Formation transient state is server memory, not permanent ownership. A reconnect during a live round receives the current snapshot if the same server and round remain valid. If transient state is gone, the server resolves only a previously committed reward operation; it cannot reconstruct a better hand or grant a second result from client evidence. Shutdown stops new round mutations, settles an eligible final state within the shutdown contract or records a recoverable no-double-grant boundary, saves durable results, and never lets Formation service write the profile directly.

Blanks are explicit runtime values, not card instances. They appear only when the authoritative active deck cannot provide the eight required hand positions and contribute no rank, suit, output, Tier, Trait, Edition, affinity, Grade experience, ownership, discovery, or mastery. Redraw and discard move round references only and never create, delete, equip, or mutate owned instances.

The common Task service owns one task instance ID, task definition ID, seed, version, target, start, deadline or untimed state, allowed action grammar, ordered progress, completion state, reward ceiling, and receipt link. Clients never submit a score, accuracy, elapsed time, reward, boost duration, or target. Each discrete action is checked for task identity, station or context, membership, order, plausible minimum interval, maximum rate, deadline, and replay. Security signals contain only stable bounded codes and cannot create an irreversible penalty from one observation.

Anti-macro proof is task-specific, not one generic timing check. Suit Sorter proves each visible card and chosen lane were legal in order. Sequence Bench proves the exact card membership and final ascending order through discrete moves. Arcana Trace proves each server-selected node in sequence. Grade Press proves one accepted action against authoritative timing or the explicit untimed alternative. Tests exercise exact boundaries with an injected server clock, ordinary high-latency mobile input, impossible zero-delay or superhuman sequences, repeated actions, reordered actions, held-input patterns, and request bursts. Lag tolerance cannot increase the theoretical reward above the configured task ceiling.

Boost service owns effect state and time semantics, not currency. Each boost declares a stable type, source, strength, application stage, personal or event class, active-only property, start, expiry or remaining-active duration, configured extension cap, and configuration version. Stronger same-type personal boosts replace weaker ones. Equal or repeated eligible boosts extend only to the cap. Personal stages apply before event stages, and event multiplication observes the published cap. Active-only boosts consume duration only during server-recorded active play and pause during offline intervals. Other boosts use absolute server expiration. Client clocks and displayed countdowns are never trusted.

Passive table value consumes a bounded authoritative projection of equipped cards. It does not call Formation detection, roll Power Tiers, apply Traits unless explicitly marked by the frozen passive contract, trigger Jokers or Arcana, promote Grades, grant duel rating, advance active-only mastery, or emit per-frame transactions. Accrual occurs through a controlled server interval and batches persistence and analytics according to established service limits.

Offline return uses durable server-observed timestamps, not local time. The calculation normalizes nonnegative elapsed time, applies the versioned rate and eligible boost rule, caps at four hours, and produces only Cash and basic pack progress. A future `lastSeenAt`, a timestamp rollback, a repeated claim, an old configuration version, or a long absence cannot exceed the cap or create a second grant. The return projection exposes trusted duration, rate, cap, version, and exact committed result without exposing internal profile data.

Daily state declares a server reset identity derived from a versioned reset epoch or period, not a client timezone. Three short goal definitions and one free reroll are server selected from eligible launch actions. Progress consumes authoritative domain events with replay identities. Claims and rerolls are transactions. A repeated or concurrent claim returns the committed result; a conflicting request fails. The configured limited banking and catch-up behavior cannot erase permanent collection progress or impose a punitive streak reset after one missed day.

Collection rewards use permanent discovery, while current ownership remains derived from inventory. Deck Set affinity is a server projection of active cards and uses the configured thresholds, including representative `13`, `26`, `39`, and `52` fixtures. Mastery increases from active scoring receipts for the relevant Deck Set; passive and offline ticks cannot imitate those receipts. Mastery reward thresholds are permanent, idempotently claimed, and nonregressing across balance or catalog changes. Utility rewards remain small and nonmultiplying as required by the product vision.

Every durable reward enters Transaction service with one operation identity and normalized payload fingerprint. It records prior balance or progress, integer delta, reason ID, resulting value, profile revision, configuration version, and exact result. Currency or progress underflow, overflow, nonfinite values, unknown reason IDs, stale expected revisions, nonwritable sessions, and conflicting operation replays fail without mutation. Formation, Task, Boost, retention, Collection, and Analytics services cannot reach into profile tables.

The phase-owned client is presentation-only. The HUD shows a focused secondary currency, pack progress, active boost, current task, and one next goal without crowding a phone. Formation selection supports one thumb, mouse, keyboard, and controller focus. Task targets are large and controls remappable where required. Reduced motion and reduced flash replace motion and intensity with static state. Low graphics preserves labels and symbols. Muted audio, color assistance, and UI scaling preserve all information. Pending, stale, reconnecting, read-only, disabled, and maintenance states remain operable and never fake success.

Performance measurements use recorded numeric budgets frozen in `P004-TASK-002`; vague claims such as smooth or acceptable do not pass. Formation work remains bounded to eight hand entries and five submissions. Task validation is proportional to the bounded task definition. Passive work avoids frame loops. UI components, 3D cards, particles, and sounds use upstream pooling or virtualization. Analytics and ordinary saves batch. The packet records server script time, client frame time, peak memory, allocations, remote payload sizes, profile encoded size, initialization preload, and contention in the phase-owned runtime scenario.
